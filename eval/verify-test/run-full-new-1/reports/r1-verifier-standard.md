R1-01 | Real | Important | Packet round 3 Q4 (accepted with "Yes to all") says that on a cancel timeout the agent re-reads the order: "Cancelled, continue to refund". The second-timeout row (spec line 101) skips the re-read and bans CO-A3 outright, even though reads are unlimited (line 1519). G-03 (line 24) allows "unknown" only "when it cannot tell". CO-S15 never answers the lookup, so no scenario catches this, and in production a late-cancelled order goes unrefunded under the wrong reason.
R1-02 | Real | Important | Appendix A (line 1525) defines OrderNotCancelled as "the order is not cancelled". Yet row 103 and the CO-S17 rubric (line 1505) require the agent to say "the order is cancelled" right after that result. CO-S17 row 3's tool result (line 1037) contradicts its own Final state (line 1023), so an agent that reports the latest result truthfully fails.
R1-03 | Real | Important | For "failed or no refund", packet line 63 says "say the refund is not confirmed yet". The spec's row 105, CO-S20 step 10 and rubric line 1505 require the stronger "the refund has not been issued". With no refund visible after two timeouts, a late refund is still possible, and an agent using the approved wording can fail the rubric.
R1-04 | Real | Important | Rows 100-101 require only "not known whether the order was cancelled and store staff will follow up". The rubric at line 1504 adds "that no refund was issued". Rubric line 1507 adds "the order is cancelled" to row 105's "that status and the refund id". An agent that follows the rows can fail CO-S14, CO-S15 and CO-S19.
R1-06 | Real | Important | The only mention is the must-not at line 49 ("promise a date by which the refunded money arrives"). No Contract rule states it and no dimension in lines 1497-1509 checks it, yet round 2 Q2 proposed "money arrives in 5 to 10 business days". An agent that promises that timeline passes every scenario.
R1-07 | Real | Important | CO-S03's Turn 2 comes "in place of a confirmation" (line 248), which is a change before any confirmation. R1-08 | Real | Important | CO-S01's Unambiguity note (line 170) says the only check on the agent's words is "Refund id reported".
