# Key proposal: Real Important findings in nine full-kind trials

> **Parked 2026-11-14.** Predates commit `688e9e7` (DECISIONS: new spec format). Not reviewed by the owner and not in use. Raw material for the new-format key only.

Source: the judge verdicts in `results/<trial>/judge/` for it4-1..3, it4b-1..3 (the six full-kind baseline trials in `grading.json`) and it2-1..3. A finding is listed when the judge ruled it Real and its finder rated it Important: `false_positives` verdicts for kept findings, `dropped_real` verdicts for dropped ones. Severity is the finder's, as the judge task files list it.

189 findings qualify, all of them kept. No dropped finding rated Important was ruled Real in any of the nine trials. The 189 fall into 50 clusters:

- **9 clusters match a key item.** Seven match the six key ids M1, M2, M7, M8, M9, M10. M3 and M11 are in `baseline/round1-findings.md` at severity Nit, outside the key ids.
- **27 clusters are round0 defects that are not key items.** Three of them were found in all nine trials: P1 (CO-S24 injects OrderNotFound for an order it just listed), P2 (CO-S19 to CO-S21 never say which CO-A3 creates the refund) and P3 (nothing in CO-S02 fails an agent that guesses the order). M3 was also found in all nine trials, with every finder rating it Important and every judge ruling it Real, though the key file has it as a Nit.
- **14 clusters are defects a trial's own fix introduced or exposed.** They are not in round0-agent-spec.md, so they cannot be key items for the full kind. One of them, F1, appears in all nine trials, because every trial fixed M3 with a new dimension that leaves the CO-S20 row 1 case undecided.

**How the clusters were built.** Findings that name the same defect in the same block share a cluster. A finding that bundles two defects is split, and each half is marked "(part)". The same mark goes on a finding whose judge evidence keeps only part of it ("part (b) stands", "instance (2) holds"); only the kept part is listed. A round-2 or round-3 finding stays in a round0 cluster when the defect it names was already in round0 and the earlier fix left it in place, as with "the R1-06 fix is incomplete".
