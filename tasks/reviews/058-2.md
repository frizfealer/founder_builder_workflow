2. **minor**, `checks.py` lines 696-711 and 731: text inside `$( )` or backticks is split away from the `git branch` call. So `git branch -D task/001 $(echo owner-idea)` is allowed when task/001 is confirmed, and bash also deletes owner-idea. A fix: refuse a forced deletion whose text holds `$(`, a backtick or `$`.
