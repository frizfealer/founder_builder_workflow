# Founder × Builder Workflow — Always-On Policies

v1 experiment: phase skills + these two always-on policies. The plan is `blueprint.html`. Each phase owns one authoritative artifact (`P0 → product-sense.md`, `P1 → agent_spec.md`, `P2 → architecture notes + build`, `P3 → observed evidence`); the shared verification mechanism is `skills/verify-artifact`.

## LIFECYCLE POLICY

1. Work only within the current phase's ownership boundary. No phase may silently redefine another phase's authoritative artifact.
2. Every normative statement in an artifact cites its source: a span in an upstream authoritative artifact, an owner confirmation, or an accepted decision in `decisions.md`.
3. When a phase needs truth it cannot source, it does not fill the gap. It leaves the truth **open in its own artifact** — a marker the artifact kind defines ("no X is established", a `rough` card, an `assumed` row) — and **reports the open truth** in the reply: what is unresolved, the span that exposed it, and which phase's owner decides. Nothing else is recorded; the open marker is the durable record and `decisions.md` is where the answer lands.
4. An open truth is answered by the owner of the phase named, never by the phase that found it. Answers go to `decisions.md` through Decision Management; the owning skill reruns for the affected scope and admits the claim with a citation to the decision.
5. After an upstream truth changes, repair only the downstream scope that consumed it, and rerun only the verification for that scope.
6. Each lifecycle phase has a stable phase-level Current Work Objective. Confirm it with the accountable human owner when entering the phase, or when the phase objective materially changes. Individual phase-skill runs do not require reconfirmation of the same phase objective. The AI may derive a narrower task focus for each run, but it must remain within the confirmed phase objective and must not narrow away commitments already established in authoritative artifacts.
7. An artifact becomes authoritative for downstream phases only after two gates close, in order: (a) **verification** — `/verify-artifact` has run against the producing skill's `VERIFY.md`; every Important finding is fixed, refuted by a verbatim source span the finder missed, or resolved by the accountable human owner — the producing agent cannot close a finding by assertion; (b) **confirmation** — the accountable human owner confirms the artifact after seeing the closed verification report. Until both close the artifact is a draft: downstream phases may read it to prepare, but any truth they take from it is unconfirmed. A scoped update reopens both gates for the changed scope only. Confirming an artifact is acceptance of what it states, including its open truths; it is not an answer to them.

## DECISION MANAGEMENT POLICY

When an open truth is reported:

1. Do not immediately ask the human.
2. Check whether the answer is already implied by an authoritative artifact or `decisions.md`.
3. Check whether an existing commitment actually needs the answer; if nothing does, the truth stays open and no one is asked.
4. If required, use the Current Work Objective and commit point to determine whether it must be resolved now.
5. Classify DROP / DEFER / RESOLVE NOW. DEFER is recorded in the artifact's open-questions section by the owning skill; DROP removes the open marker's dependents.
6. For RESOLVE NOW, respect HUMAN-OWNED / DELEGATED authority.
7. Use focused investigation only when necessary.
8. D4 Wayfinder requires the D4 gate and explicit human approval.
9. Record every accepted decision in `decisions.md`; the owning skill applies it on its next scoped run.

The full D1 tree, D3 investigation mechanics, and D4 gate details live in the `/decision-management` skill, not here.
